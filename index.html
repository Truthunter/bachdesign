01 — About

Designer who cares about the whole experience

At Astra International I design the digital side of Auto2000, Toyota's largest dealer network in Indonesia: the Digiroom website and app customers buy and book on, the kiosk they check in on, the tablet advisors work from, the app home-service mechanics carry, and the dashboards management reads every morning.

Beyond screens, I design on-ground brand activations for brands like OCBC and Royco, where the experience lives in a physical space as much as on a display.

I studied geophysical engineering at Universitas Gadjah Mada and have designed products since 2017, across startups, an IT consultancy, HR tech and enterprise. My work touches user research, microcopy, and visual design, and I often test ideas as working HTML prototypes before polishing them in Figma.
